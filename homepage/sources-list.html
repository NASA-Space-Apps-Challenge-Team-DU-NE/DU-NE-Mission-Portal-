Artemis III candidate regions

2024 lunar south pole candidate region boundaries

QuickMap’s 2024 layer shows the approximate extent of nine Artemis III candidate regions. The precise coordinate frame and boundary accuracy remain unconfirmed, so this layer cannot establish landing safety.

Region summary and boundary checks

The summary records region names, IDs and source area attributes. The validation record checks the source file hash, nine region names, polygon closure and geometry validity.

Terrain and illumination

LRO LOLA elevation and slope

The 5 m Site01 maps describe the terrain near the candidate site. Elevation and slope were checked against the original NASA cells, with wider 80 m and 240 m maps checked separately.

Lunar polar illumination maps

NASA’s 60 m maps summarize long-term sunlight, Earth visibility and permanent shadow. They provide regional context. Date-specific visibility uses separate calculations.

Sun and Earth positions

JPL Horizons tables

Hourly Sun and Earth positions for selected lunar sites. The team uses these tables to compare position calculations at matching locations and times.

JPL SPICE reference tables

The team generated a second set of position tables with SPICE. Comparing these with Horizons checks calculation consistency because both use related JPL underlying data.

Validation and context

LROC NAC shadow images

The team identified NAC products that cover Site01. Comparing predicted shadows with the images is planned.

Diviner surface temperature

Seasonal surface-temperature averages for regional context. The team checked extracted values against the source table.

Source and check records

Collection report

The project record for tracing collected files to their sources and checking the terrain files that were copied or extracted.

Recorded checks

The team’s recorded comparisons for source files and reference calculations.
