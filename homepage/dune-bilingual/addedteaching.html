Teaching resources

For teachers who want to use the Moon in class: how the Sun and Earth move in the lunar sky, and why that matters for anyone trying to land there.

In progress. The tool is still being built. This page shows what it will cover.

Planned material

  • A short explanation of why the Sun stays close to the horizon at the lunar poles.
  • Why Earth hardly moves in the Moon's sky, and why it can still drop out of sight behind a crater rim.
  • A classroom activity: pick two landing sites and decide which one a solar-powered lander should use.
  • Pictures and diagrams that can be used in slides.
