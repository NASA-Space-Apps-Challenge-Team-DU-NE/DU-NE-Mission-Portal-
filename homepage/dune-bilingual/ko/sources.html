챌린지에서 소개한 도구

LROC QuickMap
quickmap.lroc.asu.eduWeb map of Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter data. Useful for looking at sites and data layers by eye.
NASA Moon Trek
trek.nasa.gov/moonNASA's online lunar mapping portal, with terrain and image layers.
JPL Horizons System
ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizonsOnline service that gives the Sun's and Earth's positions for a chosen place and time. We use it to check our own calculations.
Canadian Space Agency: Moon exploration
www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/astronomy/moon-explorationBackground reading on lunar exploration.

달 정찰 궤도선(LRO)

NASA: Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter
science.nasa.gov/mission/lroOverview of the spacecraft whose measurements most lunar south pole data comes from.
NASA: LRO science and data
science.nasa.gov/mission/lro/science-and-dataWhat LRO measures and where its data products are kept, including LOLA heights.

지형 및 일조 데이터

PDS Geosciences Node: LRO LOLA archive
pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/lola.htmThe official archive for LOLA data. Its product documents explain how to read each file.
NASA PGDA product 78: high-resolution topography of south pole sites
pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/78Elevation and slope maps with 5 m pixels around named candidate sites, such as the Shackleton rim and Malapert Massif.
NASA PGDA product 81: south pole elevation mosaic
pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/81A single 5 m elevation map of the region around the pole. The full file is large.
NASA PGDA product 69: lunar polar illumination
pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/69Maps of how much of the time each spot is sunlit, averaged over many years. Good for choosing a region, not for a specific date.
LOLA GDRVIS product notes
ode.rsl.wustl.edu/mars/pagehelp/Content/Missions_Instruments/LRO/LOLA/GDR/GDRVIS.htmExplains maps of average sunlight and average Earth visibility over an 18.6-year cycle.
LOLA GDRSKY product notes
ode.rsl.wustl.edu/moon/pagehelp/content/missions_instruments/lro/lola/GDR/GDRSKY.htmExplains maps of how much of the sky can be seen from each spot.
NASA Lunar Surface Data Book
ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20230007818/downloads/ACD-50044%20Lunar%20Surface%20Data%20Book%20Rev%20A.pdfNASA's reference on lunar sunlight, Earth visibility and permanent shadow products, and the limits of each.
Lunar Orbital Data Explorer
ode.rsl.wustl.edu/moonSearch, map and download tool for lunar mission data.

naif.jpl.nasa.gov/naif/data_generic.htmlThe data files SPICE uses for positions, body shapes and time.
JPL NAIF: SPICE kernel guide
naif.jpl.nasa.gov/pub/naif/toolkit_docs/C/req/kernel.htmlExplains what each type of kernel file does.
JPL NAIF: azlcpo_c
naif.jpl.nasa.gov/pub/naif/toolkit_docs/C/cspice/azlcpo_c.htmlThe SPICE routine that gives azimuth and elevation as seen from a point on the surface.
JPL NAIF: subsol_c
naif.jpl.nasa.gov/pub/naif/toolkit_docs/C/cspice/subsol_c.htmlThe SPICE routine for the point directly below the Sun. Background only.

images.nasa.govSource of the photographs on these pages: art002e015231, art002e009575 and art002e014055 (Artemis II, April 2026), PIA13523 (the lunar south pole), AS11-44-6552 (Apollo 11), AS16-113-18289 (Apollo 16), PIA12909 and PIA12897 (Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter views of the Apollo 11 and 14 sites), AS12-48-7121 (Apollo 12), AS15-87-11748 (Apollo 15) and AS17-134-20435 (Apollo 17).

How we use sources

  • Cite the product's own documentation or the scientific paper, not only an archive's front page.
  • For every data file we download, record its product name, version, file name, date downloaded, map projection, reference frame, units and resolution.
  • Keep measured data, modelled products and made-up demonstration values clearly apart.
  • Do not put large mission data files in the repository; link to where they can be downloaded.
