Plan a landing

For people choosing where and when to land: compare candidate sites and dates quickly, and see straight away whether a site gets enough sunlight and can reach Earth directly.

In progress. The tool is still being built. This page shows what it will cover.

What you will be able to do

  • Pick sites from a list (Apollo sites, planned commercial landings, other countries' missions) or enter your own coordinates.
  • Choose a landing date and time, and step through the lunar day.
  • See where the Sun and Earth sit against the horizon at that site.
  • Read the numbers: Sun elevation, hours of sunlight, Earth visibility windows, and time left before sunset or Earthset.
  • Put two or more sites, or two dates at one site, side by side.

When plans change

Launches slip. When a date moves, the tool should show quickly how the same site looks on the new date, and which nearby sites still work.
